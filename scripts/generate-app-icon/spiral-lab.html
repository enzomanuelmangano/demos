Spiral Lab

The app icon's spiral is drawn from one random number. The best ones have arms drawn by close dots with wide blue gaps between them; others come out as straight rays, thick ribbons or an even dotted texture with no space. This page draws the real icon and applies the rule that throws the bad draws away. Click any icon to inspect it.

Rule thresholds

What the numbers mean

Point n sits at radius n/4 and angle n·α. What the eye reads as the spiral are the nearest neighbours: points q steps apart that land closest. They trace q arms.

Space between arms: the gap to the next arm, mid-icon. Wide gaps are the airy look; narrow ones fill the icon with an even dotted texture.

Arms: too few and the arms fuse into thick ribbons; too many and there is no space left between them.

Curve: how far the arms lean off the radius. Near 0° they are straight rays.
